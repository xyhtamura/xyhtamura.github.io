## A: Broad and conversational

Xyh Tamura is a Filipino/Ilonggo-Japanese artist, singer-songwriter, and creative technologist based in Manila. They make experimental pop, poems, browser games, web art, and software for working with sound and images. Their interests include selfhood, bodies, media, and how people live with nature and machines. Their work often brings writing, music, and code into the same project. This multicart collects five of their browser works, each with its own way of playing.

## B: Shorter

Xyh Tamura is a Filipino/Ilonggo-Japanese artist and musician based in Manila. They make songs, poems, web art, games, and odd software. ## C: More about the practice

Xyh Tamura is a Filipino/Ilonggo-Japanese artist, singer-songwriter, and creative technologist based in Manila. Their work moves between experimental pop, electronic literature, web art, games, and research. They write poems and songs, build instruments and software, and make interactive worlds. Much of this starts with questions about selfhood, bodies, and the systems people live with. The five works here bring some of those questions into listening, drawing, typing, and spending time in a place.
