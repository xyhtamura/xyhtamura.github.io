# XYH 5 IN 1 MULTICART 2026-11

Description of the local collection and its five existing works as of 2026-10-06.

## Multicart

XYH 5 IN 1 MULTICART 2026-11 is a browser menu bringing together Cornice, Ombak Lock, Foam Green City, Drawing the Hours, and Sulat for TOUCH-SCREEN. Visitors select any of the five works in any order. Each selection opens the work's existing GitHub Pages address in the same tab; browser Back returns to the menu. Each work keeps its own interface, controls, and visual design.

The menu combines a large 5 IN 1 masthead, pixel lettering, bright accents, and five numbered illustrated panels arranged as three above two, with original SVG drawings. Narrower screens use two columns or a vertical list. The palette combines paper cream, coral, amber, midnight blue, and foam green. The menu is silent. Its illustrations begin moving after six seconds without input; activity, an open information panel, or a hidden tab pauses them. A Pause motion control and reduced-motion support are available.

An About the artist button and an About this work button for each game open text panels. Their paragraph fields are empty, so the panels display “Text to come.” The collection is implemented as a live-link launcher. The five game builds remain separate, and a consolidated offline exhibition package has not been assembled.

The following descriptions reflect the existing local source versions. Those versions can contain changes beyond the published builds reached by the menu.

## Cornice

Cornice is a browser-based glyph terrarium set on a nocturnal balcony overlooking an impossible city. Plants, machines, and organisms share a small environment, with changing weather and distant city scenes. Moving through it produces terrain-specific footsteps; inspecting a cell reveals short texts and the objects occupying its layers. The encounter centers on wandering, listening, and reading the environment.

WASD or arrow keys move the player. Clicking a cell inspects it; clicking again cycles its layers. The page includes sound and display controls.

Menu destination: [Cornice](https://xyhtamura.github.io/cornice/).

## Ombak Lock

Ombak Lock is a listening puzzle in which the player tunes three lock tumblers by matching audible beating between nearby tones. Its premise draws on the deliberately differing paired tunings associated with Balinese gamelan. Each tumbler asks for a particular tuning and depth of beating, held for a specified duration. Bringing tones into unison seizes the lock.

The player starts the audio, listens to the target, and adjusts pitch and depth dials using pointer or keyboard controls. Fixed modes keep one reference tone stationary; floating modes let the player adjust the complete tone set. Six modes vary tone count, matching tolerance, hold duration, and failure cost. Sound is central to the puzzle.

Menu destination: [Ombak Lock](https://xyhtamura.github.io/ombak-lock/).

## Foam Green City

Foam Green City is a silent browser-based 3D walkthrough through procedurally arranged Filipino domestic interiors. Foam-green walls, family photographs, furniture, and household objects recur along a curved and twisting route. Seeded room generation produces familiar domestic runs interrupted by unusual geometry, larger spaces, side rooms, and passages. The local version also includes occasional covered basketball courts and open-air paths made from roofing sheets.

The visitor selects Enter and lets the walk proceed automatically, or takes control with WASD and mouse-look or dragging. Space returns to automatic walking. Pause, restart, and fullscreen controls are available. There is no navigation objective or win state. The work requires WebGL.

Menu destination: [Foam Green City](https://xyhtamura.github.io/foam-green-city/).

## Drawing the Hours

Drawing the Hours is a gesture game in which the player draws a continuous cord among moving insects and airborne seeds. Loops enclose groups, multi-pocket figures separate combinations, and ordered strokes thread creatures in sequence. Stroke length changes the cord's color and available combinations. Hazards can cut the cord or spoil a pocket.

The local campaign contains twenty-four visits, six each at morning, noon, dusk, and midnight. Each hour introduces different inhabitants, environments, and demands. Teaching and buffer visits give the player time to practice between new mechanics.

The player draws with a pointer, touch, or stylus and follows each visit's requirements. Visit cards and the Field Book explain combinations. Practice, visit selection, pause, hints, and saved campaign progress support replay. The work includes ambient sound.

Menu destination: [Drawing the Hours](https://xyhtamura.github.io/drawing-the-hours/).

## Sulat

Sulat is a typing artwork in which keys construct a landscape from thirty-six kinds of terrain. Glyphs are chosen for their shape and terrain role from Baybayin, Kana, Jawi, Devanagari, Hanzi, Thai, Telugu, Burmese, Javanese, and punctuation. Completed lines advance in whole rows during playback, change through translation and neighboring biome contact, and support animals moving over connected land or water.

Fliers appear as words whose names become readable in flight. Towns form beside water and send their names by boat. Water movement, icebergs, tides, floods, storms, and rare earthquakes alter the typed field. Animals can also change terrain or hunt other animals through authored behavior rules.

The visitor types with a physical keyboard or uses the on-screen terrain keys with a pointer or touch. Space leaves a gap; Enter completes a line. Play advances the landscape, and Pause holds it. Settings adjust alignment and the on-screen keyboard. The in-page guide explains terrain changes, animals, towns, and event markings.

Menu destination: [Sulat](https://xyhtamura.github.io/insulae-incognitae/sulat/).
