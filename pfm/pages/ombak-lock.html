Ombak Lock 2026

Acoustic tuning puzzle for one player, in the browser

An ear-training browser puzzle where a player picks combination locks by tuning acoustic interference. Players adjust the frequency of a sine-wave oscillator against a reference tone until the interference beats at a specified rate, opening each tumbler. Tuning to exact unison produces zero beats and fails the puzzle.

The game mechanics draw on ombak (wave), the intentional mistuning in Balinese gamelan where paired instruments are tuned slightly apart so unison shimmers with audible beating. The ear isolates the speed of this acoustic pulse. Players navigate frequency offsets and depth controls to match the target pulse rate, applying the auditory judgment of an ensemble tuner.
