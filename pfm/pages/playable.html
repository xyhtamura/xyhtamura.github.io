Browser terrarium

Cornice

A terrarium drawn entirely in text characters, sitting on a ledge of a city that cannot be built. Plants grow, weather passes, and the scene keeps running whether or not anyone is watching; a visitor waters, prunes, and waits.

Audio ecology

Electropond

A pond of small creatures that make the sound. A visitor disturbs the surface, drops food, and changes the salinity; the creatures flock and forage, and their speed and distance from one another drive the oscillators, filters, and stereo position you hear.
