Each runs in a browser with nothing to install, and each is linked below.

Meadow game

Drawing the Hours

A meadow visited at four hours of the day, where a player draws a line around insects and seeds. What the line encloses — a single loop, a figure-eight, two separate pockets — is what the game reads, so drawing is the move rather than clicking.

Browser terrarium

Cornice

A terrarium drawn entirely in text characters, on a ledge of a city that cannot be built. Plants grow and weather passes whether or not anyone is watching; a visitor waters, prunes, and waits.

Audio ecology

Electropond

A pond of small creatures that make the sound. A visitor disturbs the surface, drops food, and changes the salinity; the creatures' speed and distance from one another drive the oscillators, filters, and stereo position you hear.
