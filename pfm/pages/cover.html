Xyh Tamura

Artist-researcher · singer-songwriter · creative technologist

Xyh Tamura is a Filipino/Ilonggo-Japanese artist-researcher based in Manila, working across sound, electronic literature, browser instruments, installation, and participatory research. Rather than working inside supplied commercial formats, Xyh authors the apparatus: the instrument, the tuning, the notation language, or the method a community uses to tell its own story.
