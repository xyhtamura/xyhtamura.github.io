Browser game · tuning and psychoacoustics

Ombak Lock 2026

Acoustic tuning puzzle for one player, in the browser

A puzzle where the player tunes two tones against each other until they beat at a requested rate, and a lock opens. Tuning the pair to the same pitch is how you fail: at zero beats there is nothing left to hear.

Two tones a few hertz apart produce a pulse at the difference between them, and the ear hears that pulse rather than the interval. Balinese gamelan pairs instruments that are deliberately mistuned for this reason, and the shimmer the pair produces — ombak, wave — is the sound the ensemble is tuned for. The game asks for a rate rather than a pitch, so a player listens for a beat they cannot see and stops when it matches, which is the same judgment a tuner makes by ear.

Music, performance, and tools

Eosforos 2025–2026

Three-track release as xyhtochrome · Saronay, Kulintang, and Tambi by Tusa Montes · live audiovisual performance for Diana Aviado's Kaos Magick · “Theia” selected for The Wrong Eclipse

Eosforos is a three-track electronic music release and live audiovisual performance suite built with custom browser instruments. The music combines chaotic sound synthesis, microtonal vocal singing, and traditional Philippine percussion (Saronay, Kulintang, and Tambi recorded by Tusa Montes) to model the physical states of the early Hadean Earth. Live performances link audio playback directly to three custom web tools: the aperiodic oscillator Roil, the scale-free melody generator Antemelos, and the audio-reactive WebGL visualizer Hadean Flare.

Kaos Magick — the rig, the album cover, and the room.

Participatory method

Istorya sang Bȧlatyagon 2024–2025

Pilot workshop and menstrual-awareness event with the Atipan+ Gender Audit research team in Iloilo · presented at Windesheim Honours College for the Day of Action for Women's Health 2025

A participatory health research toolkit and qualitative mapping methodology developed for the Atipan+ Gender Audit in Iloilo with the University of San Agustin. The system combines workshop prompt cards, physical somatic body maps, and the Affect Atlas web application to record vernacular health terms, bodily sensations, and folk remedies across Hiligaynon, Kinaray-a, and Tagalog speech communities in Panay and Guimaras.

Iloilo — mapping against the wall of participant notes, a workshop session, and the toolkit folder.

Research, place, and installation

Tanim-Kalye 2025

Quezon City Biennial · exhibited at Carinderia Sefali, Krus na Ligas · developed with residents, the local governing unit, and botanical experts from the UP Institute of Biology

A site-specific installation and participatory research project commissioned for the Quezon City Biennial and exhibited at Carinderia Sefali in Krus na Ligas. Created with botanists from the UP Institute of Biology, local officials, and neighborhood elders, the project maps medicinal and culturally significant plants across the neighborhood street grid using high-contrast botanical film prints mounted on transparent acetate window displays.

Fieldwork — the Ligas entry and the door of the UP herbarium; the barangay hall and an alley in Krus na Ligas; two prints from a household archive, the first dated October 1969.

Browser instruments · physical simulation

Cytophones 2026

Seven browser instruments in which a two-dimensional physics simulation drives the sound

Seven instruments where a physics simulation is the sound source. A player pushes on fluid boundaries, cell membranes, flocking swarms, and stomatal pores, and the collisions and deformations drive resonators, delay lines, and waveguides in real time — so the gesture and the sound are the same event rather than a control mapped to a parameter.

Philippine timbres and environmental data feed the models: ocean swell, tidal range, and seismic telemetry perturb the membranes from outside while the player works on them from inside. They run on a phone as readily as on a laptop, which is what makes them usable in a room with other people rather than only at a desk.

Music practice

Experimental Pop and Performance 2009–2026

A solo music practice spanning songwriting, vocal performance, synthesizer programming, algorithmic composition, and visual art.

Pacing To (2015), produced with Brazilian artist MASM, is a six-track synthpop release developed through remote file exchange and mixed through cassette saturation and bandwidth limiting. Other releases include total serialism (Hyacinth), meterless vocal loops (Lie), granular synthesis (Know Me Do), vaporwave (Piyesta Plaza 1985 to 1995: Airwaves), and the Asian Composers League commission Meiosis (2009).

Browser works anyone can open

Playable works 2026

Each runs in a browser with nothing to install, and each is linked below.

Meadow game

Drawing the Hours

A meadow visited at four hours of the day, where a player draws a line around insects and seeds. What the line encloses — a single loop, a figure-eight, two separate pockets — is what the game reads, so drawing is the move rather than clicking.

Browser terrarium

Cornice

A terrarium drawn entirely in text characters, on a ledge of a city that cannot be built. Plants grow and weather passes whether or not anyone is watching; a visitor waters, prunes, and waits.

Audio ecology

Electropond

A pond of small creatures that make the sound. A visitor disturbs the surface, drops food, and changes the salinity; the creatures' speed and distance from one another drive the oscillators, filters, and stereo position you hear.

Formats, notation, and analysis

Tabota · Cycla · Stanzuary · kíkik 2025→

Open score format and notation language · recursive metrical grammar · spatial score and text editor · onset analyzer that reads a recording as a score

Tabota is a plain-text score format and JavaScript runtime that stores pitch continuously in hertz. It encodes unquantized vocal glides, microtonal inflections, Bézier pitch contours, spatial trajectories, and media triggers as temporal objects.

Cycla is a formal grammar and parser for nested, additive, and non-isochronous time structures. Compact .cyc expressions define rhythm trees and fractional phase shifts that compile into Tabota timelines.

Stanzuary is a 3D WebGL score and text editor that arranges musical events, phonemes, and text fragments across a navigable three-dimensional coordinate space.

kíkik takes a field recording and returns a score. Every micro-peak in the audio becomes a hit, and the hits drive both a swarm of MIDI notes and a granulation that drops one grain per hit. The pictured source is a cicada recorded in Diliman; kíkik is Hiligaynon for cicada, and the name is the sound.

Tools, instruments, web art 2003–2026

Three kinds of thing, built the same way. A tool makes something a person takes away — a score, an image, an audio file. An instrument is played and produces sound while it is open. A web art piece is read or inhabited rather than operated. All of them are static pages: no install, no account, no server, so each one still runs in ten years and each is one URL at xyhtamura.github.io.

Building the apparatus rather than working inside a supplied one is the practice these come out of; the residency proposal is one more of them, made from a place rather than at a desk.
