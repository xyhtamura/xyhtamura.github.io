Browser game · tuning and psychoacoustics

Ombak Lock 2026

Acoustic tuning puzzle for one player, in the browser

An ear-training browser puzzle where a player picks combination locks by tuning acoustic interference. Players adjust the frequency of a sine-wave oscillator against a reference tone until the interference beats at a specified rate, opening each tumbler. Tuning to exact unison produces zero beats and fails the puzzle.

The game mechanics draw on ombak (wave), the intentional mistuning in Balinese gamelan where paired instruments are tuned slightly apart so unison shimmers with audible beating. The ear isolates the speed of this acoustic pulse. Players navigate frequency offsets and depth controls to match the target pulse rate, applying the auditory judgment of an ensemble tuner.

Music, performance, and tools

Eosforos 2025–2026

Three-track release as xyhtochrome · Saronay, Kulintang, and Tambi by Tusa Montes · live audiovisual performance for Diana Aviado's Kaos Magick · “Theia” selected for The Wrong Eclipse

Eosforos is a three-track electronic music release and live audiovisual performance suite built with custom browser instruments. The music combines chaotic synthesis, microtonal vocals, and recorded Philippine percussion (Saronay, Kulintang, and Tambi played by Tusa Montes) to model physical transformations of the early Hadean Earth. Live performances link audio output directly to three custom web applications: the aperiodic oscillator Roil, the scale-free vocal melody generator Antemelos, and Hadean Flare, a WebGL visualizer whose domain-warped noise fields deform in real time with the audio's frequency spectrum and amplitude transients.

Kaos Magick — the rig, the album cover, and the room.

Participatory method

Istorya sang Bȧlatyagon 2024–2025

Pilot workshop and menstrual-awareness event with the Atipan+ Gender Audit research team in Iloilo · presented at Windesheim Honours College for the Day of Action for Women's Health 2025

A participatory health research toolkit and qualitative mapping methodology developed for the Atipan+ Gender Audit in Iloilo with the University of San Agustin. The system combines workshop prompt cards, physical somatic body maps, and the Affect Atlas web application to record vernacular health terms, bodily sensations, and folk remedies across Hiligaynon, Kinaray-a, and Tagalog speech communities in Panay and Guimaras.

Iloilo — mapping against the wall of participant notes, a workshop session, and the toolkit folder.

Research, place, and installation

Tanim-Kalye 2025

Quezon City Biennial · exhibited at Carinderia Sefali, Krus na Ligas · developed with residents, the local governing unit, and botanical experts from the UP Institute of Biology

A site-specific installation and participatory research project commissioned for the Quezon City Biennial and exhibited at Carinderia Sefali in Krus na Ligas. Created with botanists from the UP Institute of Biology, local officials, and neighborhood elders, the project maps medicinal and culturally significant plants across the neighborhood street grid using high-contrast botanical film prints mounted on transparent acetate window displays.

Fieldwork — the Ligas entry and the door of the UP herbarium; the barangay hall and an alley in Krus na Ligas; two prints from a household archive, the first dated October 1969.

Browser instruments · physical simulation

Cytophones 2026

Seven browser instruments in which a two-dimensional physics simulation drives the sound

A suite of seven browser instruments where two-dimensional physics simulations generate sound in real time using the Web Audio API. Players interact directly with animated physical bodies on an HTML canvas: striking circular cell membranes, plucking elastic fiber networks, perturbing fluid boundaries, and opening pressure-sensitive stomatal pores. Geometric traits, including body radius, edge tension, collision velocity, and material damping, calculate acoustic properties directly to excite modal resonators, delay lines, and digital waveguides.

Three instruments integrate live environmental telemetry alongside touch: regional marine wave data from Open-Meteo, global earthquake feeds from the USGS, and local atmospheric data from Manila perturb fluid drift and modulate resonance. Each instrument is a self-contained web page running in mobile and desktop browsers with no installation, supporting touch gestures on phones as well as mouse input.

Music practice

Experimental Pop and Performance 2009–2026

A solo music practice spanning songwriting, vocal performance, synthesizer programming, algorithmic composition, and visual art.

Pacing To (2015), produced with Brazilian artist MASM, is a six-track synthpop release developed through remote file exchange and mixed through cassette saturation and bandwidth limiting. Other releases include total serialism (Hyacinth), meterless vocal loops (Lie), granular synthesis (Know Me Do), vaporwave (Piyesta Plaza 1985 to 1995: Airwaves), and the Asian Composers League commission Meiosis (2009).

Browser works anyone can open

Playable works 2026

Each runs in a browser with nothing to install, and each is linked below.

Meadow game

Drawing the Hours

A meadow game visited at four hours of the day (dawn, noon, dusk, and midnight), where players draw continuous loops around drifting insects, seeds, and plants. The engine evaluates gesture geometry (enclosure topology, figure-eights, or partitioned loops) to trigger sound events and complete gathering tasks.

Browser terrarium

Cornice

A procedural terrarium rendered in monospaced text characters, set on an architectural ledge. Botanical growth and weather cycles run continuously; visitors water seedlings, prune overgrowth, and monitor moisture.

Audio ecology

Electropond

An interactive aquatic simulation where swimming electronic organisms generate sound through FM synthesis. Visitors disturb the water surface, add nutrients, and adjust salinity. Organism velocity, schooling density, and pairwise distance modulate oscillator frequencies, resonant filters, and stereo panning.

Formats, notation, and analysis

Tabota · Cycla · Stanzuary · kíkik 2025→

Open score format and notation language · recursive metrical grammar · spatial score and text editor · onset analyzer that reads a recording as a score

Tabota is a plain-text score format and JavaScript runtime that stores pitch continuously in hertz. It encodes unquantized vocal glides, microtonal inflections, Bézier pitch contours, spatial trajectories, and media triggers as temporal objects.

Cycla is a formal grammar and parser for nested, additive, and non-isochronous time structures. Compact .cyc expressions define rhythm trees and fractional phase shifts that compile into Tabota timelines.

Stanzuary is a 3D WebGL score and text editor that arranges musical events, phonemes, and text fragments across a navigable three-dimensional coordinate space.

kíkik is an onset-detection tool that converts field recordings into scores. Transient amplitude peaks in the audio trigger MIDI note events and a granular synthesizer that emits one grain per onset. The interface displays an analyzed recording of a Diliman cicada (kíkik in Hiligaynon, named onomatopoeically for its call).

Tools, instruments, web art 2003–2026

I author three kinds of work for the browser: tools, instruments, and web art. My tools provide graphic and video effects processors, audio production utilities, and data tools. My instruments include web-based synthesizers (such as the Anexacta physical-modeling suite), vocal organs sampled from my own voice, and real-time graphic and video synthesizers. My web art spans electronic literature, computational poetry, hypertext fiction, experimental games, and interactive web counterparts to physical pieces, including layered gelatin poems.

These works document a continuous practice of authoring custom tools, instruments, and interactive texts. The residency proposal extends this trajectory, deriving tuning models and notation directly from the local ecology of Singapore.
