Introduction

  • I’m a transdisciplinary artist, researcher, singer-songwriter, and creative technologist.
  • My work spans intermedia art, experimental pop, electronic literature, the sciences, science and technology studies (STS), theory, philosophy, and social research.
  • I build tools and instruments, make video art and games, and work in animation, musical theater, participatory mapping, and arts-based research. My tools, art practice, and research shape one another.
  • I have a BS in physics and am finishing a master’s in creative writing at the University of the Philippines. My physics thesis (PDF) modeled how light rays travel through a medium whose refractive index varies with distance from the center.
  • I’ve worked as a composer and audio engineer for over a decade, with work in film, theater, advertising, and session performance. I also work in graphics, video, writing, fashion, acting, and 3D modeling.
