# lightning man

In the locker rooms  
the floor fills up to the ankles  
with water. Reflecting  
light around his face  
and his scar. I watch  
myself by seeing  
him glance   
at me: twenty-four   
by the poolside  
of our third home  
rental, 5 p.m. after  
reading David   
Means and that lightning  
story not knowing if  
there would be anything   
left between me and my   
family, if this house   
flooded too. I can almost   
smell smoked   
leaves and skin  
I could never   
undress myself from.   
I wonder if in that moment  
he looks past  
this pupil   
free form. Nothing  
from the start.   
I’ll see you tomorrow.  
All I can say with vision.
