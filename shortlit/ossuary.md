ossuary

The signal pulses  
a dull throb   
in a desert of ground bone

the cathedral  
its spine a kind of rigging  
against a sky of quietly  
turning ochre and rust.

It breathes

A rumor of ghosts.  
The scent of lemongrass  
and burned hair.

Once, one statue held out   
what might have been a hand,   
or a bird’s wing, charred. 

It held what might have been a piece   
of fruit, or a gear  
that has stopped turning.

A case of contact  
inhibition on a scale  
of kilometers. 

No answer static  
rising from the ossuary.
