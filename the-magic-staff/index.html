Okasaki Theaterworks presents

魔杖

The Magic Staff

An Adventure Musical in Three Acts

In the depth of winter, the village of Makarata has for years been terrified by the Big Devil who steals its children. Now it falls to Jason to journey to the Lake of Light, where the Devil dwells, and win the magic staff with which, according to legend, can defeat the Evil One.

Original libretto & songs by Cy Tamura

Meralco Theater1999Three Acts · 13 Songs
壹 · I

The Story

Merging music and theater into a spectacle of joy and wonder, The Magic Staff was one of the most ambitious music-theater events of 1999 — the heroic, heartwarming adventure of Jason and his two friends, Miko and Shumpei, in search of the legendary staff that will save the children of Makarata.

It is a splendid synthesis of the art and music of Asia, produced by Okasaki Theaterworks through the expertise of Victor Henry C. Tejero (director), Jose Gentica V (composer & musical director), Nonoy Froilan (choreographer), Salvador Bernal (sets & costumes), Naomi Shoko Matsumoto (lighting), and PJ Aranador (art director).

The libretto and the full song cycle were written by Cy Tamura, then eight years old, and staged with a company of celebrated Filipino theater artists including Monique Wilson, Fides Cuyugan-Asensio, Sheila Francisco, Bernardo Bernardo, Michael Williams, and the voice of Nonoy Zuñiga.

Okasaki Theaterworks
To break the mirror
and bleed life,
to blur the terror
and bear light.

A division of Okasaki Media, Okasaki Theaterworks sought to recover the memory of Asia — to rediscover its symbols and reveal its signs — by staging original works drawn from Asian myth and legend, infusing the Asian spirit into the world of theater.
