Iso E Super

In a year I’ll be scattered in the wind, I know  
even if I memorize his name, his hair, his   
knees, and he does the same for me, that these  
memories will have  
nothing to stick to. They are not bound to any atom  
but instead, a hum emerging from arrangements of them.  
Under my breath, I pour water for both of us and he’s still    
half-asleep, getting ready for the next shift,  
a great transformation at the scale of the body,  
at least on the surface. That is my life, a simulacrum  
of something that lives a life, made up of fragments of  
weeds, that happened to form through wind   
walking the streets, gathering into a shape. There are forces that   
dissipate through chance, and arrangements through chance  
do not happen, but sometimes do, even without how   
something like, a will. The plate beside the laptop beside   
the vase near the wall, near where I want to be,  
by the bed by the chair and its end   
for a very, very long time.
