Heliotrope

we discussed the merits of positive phototaxis  
the way a life, without thinking,  
bends toward the light source.

My name is a genus I can’t quite place,  
something that blooms only at dusk.  
I have tried to classify the architecture  
of its silence: the brittle leaf  
of a note left on the counter, the shed chrysalis  
of a promise on the floor.

You move through the house  
as if letting the world pass through you,  
an earthworm’s blind contentment.  
I am left to study the evidence:  
a fingerprint on a glass,  
the pale dust of moth wing on the sill.

At night, I dream of the sea,  
of the soapfish that froths when threatened,  
of the way a body can make its own defense  
out of what is softest in itself. I wake up  
tasting salt, which is the mineral of sorrow,  
and you are still sleeping, your back to me,  
a coastline I am no longer permitted to chart.
