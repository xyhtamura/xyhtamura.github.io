survivor

Like it’s 2005, never  
watched. Or saw who  
or in what island we voted  
on, or if it was just easier.  
or if it was to just to show   
off how well we could finally  
render water and glass.  
A fantasy of the tropics  
rolling, rolling on the edge  
of a dense star’s accretion  
disk, there: balatik  
and moroporo, which means   
it’s time  
to fish or gather      
ourselves. Here we are. We  
are here, behind  
heat and water  
behind constellations.  
No place, no sea
