Perihelion

when you loved 
you thought
once 
of marriage
you thought about the ball
that bounced 
over the fence
you would never find again 
in your youth.

Is it wrong 
in a way
to wish to have 
met then? a few years earlier?
or two or three years?
Droplets that 
slide off the side 
of your head;
a golden heat.

There is gravity that holds
life in this planet
there are always those 
mornings when you feel filled, sick 
with the revulsion 
of your need
for something like memory.

Now you try
not to think about it. You are ready
for tonight's comet.
You were not born 
the last time it was
visible. But really,
comets don't ask 
wishes. And yet, 
there is gravity.
