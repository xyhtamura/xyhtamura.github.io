Xyh Tamura

Artist-researcher · singer-songwriter · creative technologist

Xyh Tamura is a Filipino/Ilonggo-Japanese transdisciplinary artist-researcher, singer-songwriter, and creative technologist based in Manila, working through intermedia art, experimental pop, electronic literature, and participatory research. Their practice links songwriting, videopoetry, and installation with technical development: browser instruments, physical-modelling synthesizers, microtonal notation, and client-side audio and video processors. They hold a BS in Physics, cum laude, from UP Diliman (Theoretical Physics), and the instruments here are built from the models that describe them: waveguides, ray tracing, memristor histories, fluid simulation, and two-dimensional physics.

Browser instruments · physical simulation

Cytophones 2026

Seven browser instruments in which a two-dimensional physics simulation drives the sound

A suite of seven browser instruments where two-dimensional physics simulations generate sound in real time using the Web Audio API. Players interact directly with animated physical bodies on an HTML canvas: striking circular cell membranes, plucking elastic fiber networks, perturbing fluid boundaries, and opening pressure-sensitive stomatal pores. Geometric traits, including body radius, edge tension, collision velocity, and material damping, calculate acoustic properties directly to excite modal resonators, delay lines, and digital waveguides.

Three instruments integrate live environmental telemetry alongside touch: regional marine wave data from Open-Meteo, global earthquake feeds from the USGS, and local atmospheric data from Manila perturb fluid drift and modulate resonance. Each instrument is a self-contained web page running in mobile and desktop browsers with no installation, supporting touch gestures on phones as well as mouse input.

Field recording · found score · voice

kíkik 2026

Electroacoustic piece and the tool that made it · an onset analyzer and hit-seeded granulator, audio in and MIDI and audio out

The piece uses a recording of the cicada Platypleura fulvigera made outside a former home near Diliman in 2016. The tool treats every transient peak in the recording as a hit. Each hit triggers a MIDI note and releases one grain cut from a multilingual Ilonggo, Japanese, and English poem, so the cicada's attacks set the timing of the voice.

Kíkik is the Hiligaynon word for cicada. In Filipino and Japanese cultures cicadas mark the season, the hour, and the heat of a particular place. The sung third line quotes Dandansoy, a traditional Ilonggo folk song about leaving, which the cicada chorus carries along with the rest of the poem.

Ray tracing · room acoustics

Gliese 2025–2026

A multi-tap delay and reverb generator whose taps come from tracing sound rays through a model atmosphere

The refractive-index profile has a sound channel at its axis. Rays launched from a point source are integrated with the velocity Verlet method and reflect at an inner and an outer boundary. A tap is recorded each time a ray passes through the receiver, with its gain set by geometric spreading and reflection loss, and the taps are scaled so that the first arrival lands on a chosen delay.

The model comes from my 2012 undergraduate thesis at the National Institute of Physics, UP Diliman, on ray propagation in a medium with a spherically symmetric index of refraction, treated through the Lagrangian formulation (adviser Jose Perico H. Esguerra).

Memristor synthesizer · history of science

Physa · Biomemristor as Instrument 2026

A browser instrument, and the extended abstract (September 2026) on the contested device it is built from

Physa is a monophonic browser instrument and input processor. A charge-controlled memristor, simulated in code, sits in the audio path, and a parallel RC circuit holds the capacitive behavior. With senescence enabled, frequency bands feed a shared wear map that changes the resistance curve the next sound meets, so a voice or a played note alters the instrument's later response.

Biomemristor as Instrument follows one term through three fields. In 2015 the slime mould Physarum polycephalum was reported as a biological memristor, and about a decade of computer music was built on that claim. A 2025 characterisation of 310 observations from 38 cultures found no pinched loops and attributed the elliptical curves to capacitance. The paper specifies Physa from that dispute, with the memristor model and the RC model as separate sounding circuits that can be compared in performance.

Digital waveguide · electroacoustic theory

The Unbounded Organ · Composition Without Audition 2026

A browser organ model with no range restriction, and the abstract (September 2026) on composing work that cannot be heard

The Unbounded Organ models an organ pipe as a delay line of length 2L/c with reflection signs at its ends, so the mode series comes from the physics and not from a chosen number of partials. The same specification runs from 8.575 millimeters to 40,075 kilometers. At a few meters the output is a note, at tens of kilometers it is a diffuse echo, and at Earth's radius the fundamental is 26.92 µHz, a period of 10.32 hours.

Composition Without Audition takes the inaudible end of that range as a compositional result. Some outputs cannot be reproduced by a loudspeaker or stored in a file, yet a render and a computational score remain available. The work then sits in the apparatus of models, formats, and media, and listening becomes one possible realization.

Ambient display · Raspberry Pi

Deskarium 2026

A sound-responsive ASCII marine window on a 1024 × 600 desk panel driven by a Raspberry Pi 4B. It runs on its own clock and the room's sound modulates it: fish leave the frame in quiet and file back in when there is noise. Input is three buttons and a rotary encoder, with a microphone and a speaker, and the video shows the audio menu being calibrated on the device.

Video synthesizer · fluid simulation

Benzaiten 2026

A video instrument whose image is the read-out of a live 2D fluid simulation, with semi-Lagrangian advection, curl-noise stirring, and pressure projection on a 256² GPU grid. The substrate can be a procedural color field, an image, a looping video, or a webcam feed, and Web Audio FFT analysis couples the sound in the room to the flow.

Research, place, and installation

Tanim-Kalye 2025

Quezon City Biennial · exhibited at Carinderia Sefali, Krus na Ligas · developed with residents, the local governing unit, and botanical experts from the UP Institute of Biology

A site-specific installation and participatory research project commissioned for the Quezon City Biennial and exhibited at Carinderia Sefali in Krus na Ligas. Created with botanists from the UP Institute of Biology, local officials, and neighborhood elders, the project maps medicinal and culturally significant plants across the neighborhood street grid using high-contrast botanical film prints mounted on transparent acetate window displays.

Fieldwork — the Ligas entry and the door of the UP herbarium; the barangay hall and an alley in Krus na Ligas; two prints from a household archive, the first dated October 1969.

Non-causal media processing

Hindcasts 2025–2026→

Hindcasts is a suite of offline audio and video tools that process a static file as a complete span, operating in both temporal directions before events arrive.

Remanence

An offline audio and video processor that simulates magnetic tape print-through and tape-spool magnetic hysteresis.

Metachamber

An offline acoustic reverberator that measures forthcoming gaps between notes to tailor reverb decay lengths exactly to future silences.

Pythia

An acausal granular delay and sidechain processor with negative delay, lookahead ducking, and pitch spray.

Prolepsis

An offline video feedback generator that renders anticipatory optical particle trails and precursor echoes prior to visual motions.

Sounder

A multi-band dynamic audio processor that computes time-occupancy amplitude histograms across an entire audio file, letting users redraw custom compression curves without threshold attack/release pumping.

CyberScotoma

An offline video glitch processor that freezes localized screen cells and populates them with image data sampled from future video frames.

The Commitments of Physical Modeling

Analyzes waveguide and modal synthesis instruments as formal theories of timbre. Each algorithm commits to assumptions about damping, excitation, and acoustic geometry, and the source code records those commitments.

Night Bus

An electroacoustic song and a paper on its rhythm, presented at Saliksik-Musika II, UP, June 2025. The paper analyzes how Tagalog rowing songs (soliranin) and vendor chants (indolanin) persist as metric structures in Metro Manila commuting.

From Interiority to Interaction

Article in Philosophy & Digitality (2025), presented at Denkwerkstatt Berlin, August 2024. Drawing on AIBO robot funerals, the android priest Mindar, and Geminoid androids, it shows interiority to be a relational effect of social ritual and framing.
