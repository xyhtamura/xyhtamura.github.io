Ambient display · Raspberry Pi

Deskarium 2026

A sound-responsive ASCII marine display on a 1024 × 600 desk panel driven by a Raspberry Pi 4B. The simulation runs on an internal clock modulated by room acoustics: sustained vocalizations or sounds raise a collective interest variable that retains fish within the frame, whereas extended silence allows them to swim out past the boundary. Hardware controls comprise three buttons and a rotary encoder with an onboard microphone and speaker; the video demonstrates threshold calibration in the audio menu.

Video synthesizer · fluid simulation

Benzaiten 2026

A real-time video synthesizer driven by a two-dimensional fluid simulation executed on a 256² GPU grid. The engine computes semi-Lagrangian velocity advection, curl-noise vorticity stirring, and pressure projection in WebGL shaders. Source textures—procedural color fields, static images, video loops, or live webcam feeds—are advected through the velocity field, while Web Audio FFT analysis couples room sound directly to fluid forces.
