Ray tracing · room acoustics

Gliese 2025–2026

A multi-tap delay and reverb generator deriving its delay taps from sound rays traced through a model atmosphere

A multi-tap delay and acoustic reverberation tool that derives reflection schedules by tracing sound rays through a model planetary atmosphere. The refractive-index profile defines an axial acoustic sound channel. Rays launched from a point source are numerically integrated using the velocity Verlet method, reflecting between concentric inner and outer boundaries. Each receiver intersection records a delay tap whose gain accounts for geometric spreading and boundary absorption, scaled so the first arrival matches a specified pre-delay.

The acoustic model derives from undergraduate thesis research at the National Institute of Physics, UP Diliman (2012), on ray propagation in spherically symmetric refractive media treated through a Lagrangian formulation (adviser Jose Perico H. Esguerra).
