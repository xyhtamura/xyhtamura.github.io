The Commitments of Physical Modeling 2026

International Conference on Timbre · Université de Montréal · July 2026

Analyzes digital waveguide and modal synthesis implementations as formal theories of timbre. Each synthesis algorithm embeds physical assumptions regarding boundary damping, mechanical excitation, and resonator geometry, which source code records as executable commitments.

Research · philosophy of technology

From Interiority to Interaction 2024–2025

Presented at Denkwerkstatt Berlin, August 2024 · published in Philosophy & Digitality, 2025

A journal article analyzing how human communities attribute personhood and agency to synthetic entities. Drawing on field studies of Sony's AIBO robot memorial services in Japanese temples, the android priest Mindar at Kōdai-ji, and Geminoid androids, the paper argues that attributed interiority is a relational effect produced through interaction framing and social ritual rather than an intrinsic property of a machine.
