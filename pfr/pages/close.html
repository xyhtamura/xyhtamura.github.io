The Commitments of Physical Modeling

Analyzes digital waveguide and modal synthesis implementations as formal theories of timbre. Each synthesis algorithm embeds physical assumptions regarding boundary damping, mechanical excitation, and resonator geometry, which source code records as executable commitments.

Night Bus

An electroacoustic song and musical analysis presented at Saliksik-Musika II, UP Diliman, June 2025. The paper analyzes how historical Tagalog rowing songs (soliranin) and vendor calls (indolanin) survive as rhythmic structures in contemporary Metro Manila transit.

From Interiority to Interaction

Article in Philosophy & Digitality (2025), presented at Denkwerkstatt Berlin, August 2024. Drawing on AIBO robot memorial services, the android priest Mindar, and Geminoid androids, the paper argues that attributed interiority is a relational effect of interaction framing and social ritual rather than an intrinsic property.
