Field recording · found score · voice

kíkik 2026

Electroacoustic piece and browser tool · audio onset analyzer and hit-seeded granulator with MIDI and audio output

The piece and software derive from a 2016 field recording of the cicada Platypleura fulvigera made in Diliman. The software analyzes the audio for transient micro-peaks to extract a found score. Each detected attack triggers a MIDI note whose pitch follows the local spectral centroid, while releasing a grain from a spoken poem in Hiligaynon, Japanese, and English, locking vocal articulation to the insect rhythm.

Kíkik is the Hiligaynon name for cicada. In Panay and Luzon, as in Japan, cicadas serve as vernacular acoustic markers of season, hour, and temperature. The composition's sung melody quotes Dandansoy, a traditional Ilonggo song of departure, carried across the granular synthesis alongside the cicada chorus.
