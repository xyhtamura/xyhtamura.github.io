Field recording · found score · voice

kíkik 2026

Electroacoustic piece and the tool that made it · an onset analyzer and hit-seeded granulator, audio in and MIDI and audio out

The piece uses a recording of the cicada Platypleura fulvigera made outside a former home near Diliman in 2016. The tool treats every transient peak in the recording as a hit. Each hit triggers a MIDI note and releases one grain cut from a multilingual Ilonggo, Japanese, and English poem, so the cicada's attacks set the timing of the voice.

Kíkik is the Hiligaynon word for cicada. In Filipino and Japanese cultures cicadas mark the season, the hour, and the heat of a particular place. The sung third line quotes Dandansoy, a traditional Ilonggo folk song about leaving, which the cicada chorus carries along with the rest of the poem.
