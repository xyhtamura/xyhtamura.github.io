Lamp for Stilling the Sun

This instrument flashes across the screen at 4–18 Hz. Flashing light can cause seizures, migraine, dizziness, nausea, or other physical reactions.

Do not use this instrument if you have a seizure disorder, photosensitivity, or sensitivity to flashing light, or if you feel unwell or unsure. Stop immediately if you feel discomfort.

Use this instrument with your eyes closed. Face the light, close your eyes, then start it. Do not look directly at the flashing field.
