Xyh Tamura

Artist-researcher · singer-songwriter · creative technologist

Xyh Tamura is a Filipino/Ilonggo-Japanese artist-researcher based in Manila. The practice investigates voice, tuning, notation, and interface across experimental music, electronic literature, browser instruments, installation, and participatory research. Rather than working inside supplied commercial formats, Xyh authors the apparatus: the acoustic instrument, notation language, tuning model, file format, or participatory method.

xyhnthesizer — Kontakt vocal organ sampled from Xyh's singing

Desiderata, 2026.

Desiderata is an ambient vocal album built entirely from recorded acoustic voice and breath. Tracks layer time-stretched, granulated vocalizations and microtonally vocoded passages against ocean and insect field recordings. The companion xyhnthesizer is a Kontakt vocal organ sampled directly from the tracking sessions, mapping sustained phonemes and breath registers across polyphonic keyboard splits.

Music, performance, and tools

Eosforos 2025–2026

Three-track release as xyhtochrome · Saronay, Kulintang, and Tambi by Tusa Montes · live audiovisual performance for Diana Aviado's Kaos Magick · “Theia” selected for The Wrong Eclipse

Eosforos is a three-track electronic music release and live audiovisual performance suite built with custom browser instruments. The music combines chaotic sound synthesis, microtonal vocal singing, and traditional Philippine percussion (Saronay, Kulintang, and Tambi recorded by Tusa Montes) to model the physical states of the early Hadean Earth. Live performances link audio playback directly to three custom web tools: the aperiodic oscillator Roil, the scale-free melody generator Antemelos, and the audio-reactive WebGL visualizer Hadean Flare.

Research · philosophy of technology

From Interiority to Interaction 2024–2025

Presented at Denkwerkstatt Berlin, August 2024 · published in Philosophy & Digitality, 2025

A journal article analyzing how human communities attribute personhood and agency to synthetic entities. Drawing on field studies of Sony’s AIBO robot funerals in Japanese temples, the android priest Mindar at Kōdai-ji, Geminoid telepresence androids, and conversational language models, the paper shows that interiority is a relational effect produced through social rituals and communicative framing rather than an intrinsic property of a machine.

The Commitments of Physical Modeling 2026

International Conference on Timbre · Université de Montréal · July 2026

A conference paper analyzing digital waveguide and modal synthesis instruments as formal theories of timbre. The paper demonstrates how synthesis algorithms formalize assumptions about material damping, excitation mechanics, and acoustic geometry, treating instrument source code as a set of aesthetic commitments that define what a performer can express.

Xyh Tamura5

Formats and spatial notation

Tabota · Cycla · Stanzuary 2025→

Open score format and notation language · recursive metrical grammar · spatial score and text editor

Tabota is a plain-text score format and JavaScript runtime that stores pitch continuously in hertz. It encodes unquantized vocal glides, microtonal inflections, Bézier pitch contours, spatial trajectories, and media triggers as temporal objects.

Cycla is a formal grammar and parser for nested, additive, and non-isochronous time structures. Compact .cyc expressions define rhythm trees and fractional phase shifts that compile into Tabota timelines.

Stanzuary is a 3D WebGL score and text editor that arranges musical events, phonemes, and text fragments across a navigable three-dimensional coordinate space.

Night Bus is an electroacoustic song and performance paper from the intermedia suite Of Another Shore. Digital waveguide · physical modeling

The Unbounded Organ 2026

A browser physical-modeling synthesizer that simulates acoustic open and stopped organ pipes across lengths from 8.575 millimeters to 40,075 kilometers. By calculating delay-line waveguides across extreme dimensions, the instrument transitions continuously from high audible pitches down to diffuse echoes and planetary infrasonic cycles.

Acoustic puzzle · web audio

Ombak Lock 2026

An ear-training audio puzzle where players pick a combination lock by listening to acoustic interference. Inspired by the intentional mistuning (ombak) of Balinese gamelan pairs, players adjust frequency dials to find and hold specific acoustic beating rates rather than tuning to exact unison.

Non-causal media processing

Hindcasts 2025–2026→

Hindcasts is a suite of offline audio and video tools that process a static file as a complete span, operating in both temporal directions before events arrive.

Remanence

An offline audio and video processor that simulates magnetic tape print-through and tape-spool magnetic hysteresis.

Metachamber

An offline acoustic reverberator that measures forthcoming gaps between notes to tailor reverb decay lengths exactly to future silences.

Pythia

An acausal granular delay and sidechain processor with negative delay, lookahead ducking, and pitch spray.

Prolepsis

An offline video feedback generator that renders anticipatory optical particle trails and precursor echoes prior to visual motions.

Sounder

A multi-band dynamic audio processor that computes time-occupancy amplitude histograms across an entire audio file, letting users redraw custom compression curves without threshold attack/release pumping.

CyberScotoma

An offline video glitch processor that freezes localized screen cells and populates them with image data sampled from future video frames.

Xyh Tamura19

Electronic literature · intermedia suite

Of Another Shore 2025→

Poems, songs, videopoems, simulations, and browser instruments in one scrolling panorama

An intermedia web artwork and electronic literature suite structured as a single continuous scrolling panorama. Visitors navigate through five vertical ecological zones (sky, sea, shore, shelf, strata) containing interactive objects that launch individual poems, video works, and simulations. The surrounding interface adopts 1990s personal website aesthetics with a visitor counter, guestbook, legend, and margin encyclopedia.

Each embedded piece retains independent interactive controls: The World provides a real-time N-body gravitational orbit simulator with variable gravity and spin parameters; Dream Window Return renders draggable text and image windows; and body clocks compares 72 Japanese microseasons with concurrent Philippine climatic events and internet culture history.

Electronic literature · digital periodic table

Table of Metalloids 2025→

Seven elements, seven constraints

An electronic literature collection structured as an interactive periodic table of the seven chemical metalloids (boron, silicon, germanium, arsenic, antimony, tellurium, polonium). Clicking any element displays its physical and chemical properties alongside experimental prose, slipstream fiction, and kinetic poetry written under strict material and toxicological constraints.
