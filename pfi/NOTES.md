2026-09-01 — Claude Code — Rebuilt the landscape layout around the print portfolio's no-crop invariant and gave the slides a solved row break.

**What was wrong.** The landscape block spent its height budgets as fixed `height: Nvh` with `aspect-ratio: auto` and `object-fit: cover`, which is the one arrangement `portfolio.css` warns against. Measured at 1280 x 720: 88 of 114 pictures were rendered in a box disagreeing with the picture's own ratio, the worst showing 13% of its frame (`p02-c` in a 5.96:1 box against a 0.80:1 picture, and `p04-c` at 6.82:1 against 1.00:1). Panelled sheets filled 48–59% of the slide height with pictures covering 10–17% of it, and pages 7, 13, 14, and 15 overflowed. Twelve figures also carried an inline millimetre `max-height` from the print build — a ceiling with no matching width ceiling, which is the other way a box comes out the wrong shape.

**What it is now.** One invariant, stated once in `styles.css`: a filled slot takes `aspect-ratio: var(--ar)`, so the box is the picture, and every budget is a `max-height` with a matching `max-width` of budget times ratio. Landscape then has two shapes and no others — a single work as pictures beside text across the slide, several works as a stack of panels each holding a picture beside its own text — and both fill the slide rather than its top half. Height budgets are read off the laid-out box with container query units, so a page header or a late font swap changes the number with nothing measured in JavaScript. Portrait keeps the print flow; only the ceilings changed there.

**The row break is solved, not authored.** A row of pictures shown whole across width W solves to a height of W over the sum of its ratios, so guessing where to break it decides how much of the slide the pictures cover — three wide pictures in one row reached a fifth of the height they were given, five tallish ones in three rows a third of the width. `planRows` in `app.js` instead finds, for each row count, the best contiguous partition by dynamic programming, and takes the count covering the most picture area; `fillMedia` then sizes the picture track to what that plan asks for and solves the plan again against the track it got. Two passes, not a loop. Nothing in it is tuned to these pictures: swap one for another shape and the layout re-solves.

**Verified** by measuring rendered boxes against `naturalWidth`/`naturalHeight` for all 114 images on all 20 slides, at 1240 x 700, 1280 x 720, 1280 x 600, 1100 x 620, 1024 x 640, 900 x 560, 860 x 520, and 820 x 620 landscape, and 760 x 1180 and 700 x 1000 portrait. Every box now agrees with its picture to within 1%, which is subpixel rounding. In landscape every slide is exactly the scroll container's height (no slide overflow at any size tested), no document-level horizontal overflow, and pictures cover 34–44% of the slide against 10–27% before. Portrait: crops went from 18 to 0 and total across-slide overflow from 14,377 px to 5,982 px, though pages 2, 7, 14, and 15 scroll further than they used to now that their pictures are whole. The rebuild path was exercised in both directions and unpicks to the 16 authored `.justified` rows in portrait and rebuilds 9 spreads, 10 panelled sheets, and the cover stack in landscape. The image viewer, the direct work links (37 links, 27 titles), and hash deep-linking were exercised directly. `node --check` passes on `app.js` and `scripts/layout-audit.js`. The measurement harness is kept at `scripts/layout-audit.js` rather than left in a scratch folder.

**Not verified, and why.** The slide counter and title are driven by an IntersectionObserver, and neither that nor `requestAnimationFrame` is delivered while the browser pane's frame loop is paused, which it was throughout — a freshly constructed IntersectionObserver on the same root also never fired, so this is the environment and not the page. The counter therefore reads stale in every measurement above. `setActive`, `goTo`, and the observer block are byte-identical to the previous version, checked by diff, so nothing here changed them. The same throttling means the `ResizeObserver` that triggers a rebuild could not be observed firing; the rebuild itself was exercised through the `resize` listener, which shares its whole path, and three signals are registered (`ResizeObserver` on the slide area, `resize`, and the media query's `change`) because the latter two were both seen not to fire under an emulated viewport.

**Undone.** Portrait is still the print document's flow, so pages 8, 13, 19, and 20 scroll within their slide; whether portrait should be rebuilt the way landscape now is, is the open question and the next step. A `.panels.split` sheet whose two works are each one wide picture — pages 10 and 11 — is bounded at about 27% picture area by the stacked shape itself, since each picture can only be half the slide tall; laying those two panels side by side instead would gain roughly half again, and was not done because the stacked shape is what was asked for. The thumbstrip weighting that kept supporting pictures smaller than lead ones in the print document is gone from landscape: the area solver treats every row alike, which is what doubled the picture area on pages 2 and 14, at the cost of that hierarchy. `pfi` had no ROADMAP.md entry despite site subfolders like `landline` and `buddhist-traditions` having them; one was added and the Published Tools and Creative & Art Tools counts raised to 16 and 35.
