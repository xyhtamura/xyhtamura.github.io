Harvests Happen

2026 · looping video, 15 seconds

A goblin drawn in crayon sits on a stool in a green room. It breathes, rubs its face, stretches its legs, and reaches into the leaves of a pressed plant that grows out of a rock beside it. The room's crayon strokes are redrawn eight times a second, and paper insects, then a school of paper fish, pass through. The scene is drawn live in the browser, and the picture and the music loop together every 15 seconds.

The music is a phrase in 7/8 at 56 beats per minute, tuned to 19-tone equal temperament.
