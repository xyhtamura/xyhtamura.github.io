Xyh Tamura

Artist-researcher · instrument builder · composer

Xyh Tamura is a Filipino/Ilonggo-Japanese artist-researcher and composer based in Manila. The practice investigates voice, notation, physical modeling, and detection thresholds across experimental music, browser instruments, and electronic literature. The work builds instruments that declare their own limits, and report their own artifacts beside the physics those artifacts imitate. Rather than working inside supplied commercial formats, Xyh authors the apparatus: the acoustic instrument, notation language, tuning model, or verification network.

Digital waveguide · physical modeling

The Unbounded Organ 2026

One waveguide specification evaluated across pipe lengths from 8.575 mm to 40,075 km · limits matrix and threshold readout

The Unbounded Organ evaluates one waveguide specification at any pipe length from millimetres to the Earth's circumference (8.575 mm to 40,075 km), rendered sample by sample at whatever rate it produces. The same model gives a musical pitch at human scale, a diffuse echo at room scale, and an ultra-slow drift lasting hours at planetary radius.

The instrument declares its own physical and representational boundaries. A five-limit matrix separates the atmospheric column's cutoff frequency from the digital medium's Nyquist and memory bounds, reporting fractional-delay interpolator phase dispersion beside physical end correction. The readout names which threshold a fundamental failed, by how much in octaves, and which harmonic mode still clears the acoustic cutoff.

Optical media · microfacet transmission

Aeropane 2026

Frosted glass, gel, reeded glass, and grown ice modeled as a pane between the viewer and the image · offline progressive render

A rough dielectric interface is treated as a microfacet transmission lobe, which separates into two channels on the image behind the pane. The mean surface normal tilts the transmitted ray, offsetting the image by about (n−1)·∇h·d for a height field h at pane-to-subject distance d. Roughness widens the lobe into a spatially varying blur. Any imported image can serve as the height field, so one picture becomes the optical surface through which another is seen, which is the point at which the filter becomes an instrument.

Scanning standards · atmospheric channel

W2XDX 2026

The receiving end of a television standard that never shipped, pulled in when the atmospheric duct opens

The raster is one space-filling curve among many, and the curve decides how a channel disturbance appears in space. A delayed echo is a shift of τ samples along the one-dimensional signal; the scan standard maps signal position to screen position, so the same echo lands as a ghost under a raster, a local blur under a Hilbert ribbon, and scattered speckle under a seeded walk. Measured spatial roughness across the three rises 0.025, 0.131, 0.165. A simulated atmosphere is the channel under every standard, so the picture reports the air it crossed as much as the signal that was sent.

Detector aperture · particle game

Catchment 2026

Browser particle catcher, running also on a Raspberry Pi desk panel

The catcher is a detector aperture with three settings, and it accepts a particle only when charge, parity and spin all agree. A run is a sequence of decisions about what to be ready for, and whatever the player was not ready for passes through uncaught.

The magnetic field bends each track by momentum over charge, so the curve says what a particle is before its letter is legible. Detection begins with a prior commitment about what can be caught, which is the same order of operations a trigger imposes on a real detector: the configuration is fixed before the event arrives, and everything the configuration excludes leaves no record.

Live planetary data · browser instruments

Cytophones 2026

Seven single-file browser instruments · marine, seismic, and weather feeds read at run time

Each cytophone maps the shape, motion, contact, and environment of animated bodies into sound: tuning is morphology. Three of the seven take their environment from live instrument networks. Halocline reads wave height, period, and current velocity from a marine feed and turns them into harmonic detuning, spectral tilt, and the resonant breathing of submerged bodies. Seismocyte scatters six mineral friction archetypes across a stereo field from the USGS earthquake feed. Murmuration and stomata take Manila temperature, humidity, wind, and condition.

These are the works where the sensing is somebody else's. A buoy, a seismometer array, and a weather station are read over the network and arrive already reduced to numbers, and the instrument sounds whatever those numbers say. Each piece is one HTML file carrying its own renderer and Web Audio engine, so the whole chain from a public feed to a pitch is open to reading.

Three of the seven: a body detail, murmuration on live Manila weather, and cambium’s node-and-fiber graph.

Network poetics · DSP synthesis

a landline imagining a line probe during a storm 2026

ITU-T V.34 DSP handshake and line-probe degradation simulation

A telephone line probe read as a sensing instrument. Two negotiating modems measure channel attenuation, phase jitter, and harmonic distortion across an active storm, and the handshake is the part that carries the argument: before anything can be sent, the two ends have to agree on what the line will carry, while the storm keeps changing the answer.

A monitoring network is infrastructure whose output is agreement rather than raw data, which makes the V.34 handshake a small ancestor of multi-detector coincidence and of the work of characterising a glitch. The piece was written as a score and a renderer together; the reference implementation and the build notes are published beside it.

Non-causal media processing

Hindcasts 2025–2026→

Hindcasts is a suite of offline audio and video tools that process a static file as a complete span, operating in both temporal directions before events arrive.

Remanence

Models magnetic print-through and acausal hysteresis across wound tape reels, for audio and video.

Metachamber

An offline reverb that maps release-to-onset silences, fitting decay tails to future onset gaps with masking credit.

Pythia

An acausal granular delay and sidechain processor with negative delay, lookahead ducking, and pitch spray.

Prolepsis

Video feedback computed across a whole clip, with the time arrow free: a causal wake, an anticipatory precursor swarm ramping into each transient, or both at once, zero-phase.

Sounder

Dynamics without detection. It reads the file, builds the time-occupancy histogram over level, and hands back the transfer curve to redraw. One window control walks from memoryless waveshaping to whole-piece normalization, per frequency band.

CyberScotoma

A scotoma is a hole in the visual field; a migraine aura is a hole that arrives before the pain it announces. Held cellular regions stop refreshing and fill with donor content drawn from later in the clip, so the future bleeds backward.

Reading the whole file before starting makes causality a property of the apparatus rather than of the events. Where a real-time processor commits at each sample and keeps only what it decided to keep, these tools hold the span and can shape a moment against what follows it. Sounder stands furthest from detection: it builds a transfer curve from the time-occupancy histogram of the whole piece, so nothing has to be recognised as an event for the dynamics to change.

Xyh Tamura8

Planetary acoustics · ray tracing delay

Gliese 2025–2026

Spherical acoustic ray tracing in graded-index atmospheres · Earth SOFAR channel and exoplanet presets

Gliese models acoustic wave propagation through graded-index planetary atmospheres using spherical ray tracing. Refraction gradients bend acoustic paths into waveguiding sound channels, deriving multi-tap delays and reverberation characteristics directly from the medium's density and temperature profiles.

The medium decides what propagates: an Earth SOFAR deep-ocean preset sits beside three exoplanet-atmosphere models (Gliese 581g, Kepler 186f, HD 189733b). The same source signal reaches the receiver or is trapped, absorbed, or dispersed depending on the planetary column it was launched into, turning atmospheric refraction into an audible spatial transfer function.

Equation-first synthesis

Anexacta 2026→

Five browser instruments on one mathematical-definition engine · Aliquoto, Cella, Moire, Physa, Spolium

Aliquoto

Writes additive partial distributions across just-intonation ratios and geometric series.

Cella

Excites resonant spectra by propagating two-dimensional waves across coupled cellular grids.

Moire

Produces spectra through phase-modulation interference, mapping spatial frequencies to microtonal sidebands.

Physa

Drives a charge-controlled memristor whose non-volatile state carries hysteresis between evaluations.

Each instrument states its model as an equation the player can edit, so a sound follows from a written definition instead of from a stored preset. The five share one definition engine, which is what lets a spectrum written in Aliquoto be excited in Cella and quoted by Spolium.

Physa was the first member to carry state between evaluations, which changes what a definition can mean: the same expression evaluated twice gives different results because the element remembers. Spolium reads a quoted spectrum through equation-driven read heads with continuous cubic Hermite resampling.

Archival disaster footage under blackout redaction

Archival footage of the 1990 Luzon, 2011 Tōhoku, and 2020 Taal disasters is held behind a redaction that admits only part of each frame. Video synthesis · simulated fluid

Benzaiten 2026

A 2D fluid simulation on a GPU grid, read out as the picture

The image is the read-out of a fluid simulation rather than a composition laid over one. Choosing a regime changes the physics under the picture — magnetohydrodynamic, viscoelastic, or a noise warp running no simulation at all — and the same substrate then reports a different state. Touch and audio drive the field, so watching it is closer to reading an instrument than to watching a rendering.

Formats and spatial notation

Tabota · Cycla 2025→

Open score format and notation language · continuous pitch representation · recursive metrical grammar

MIDI's 128 semitone bins write a sung glide to the nearest legal value and the glide never enters the record; Tabota is a plain-text score format and JavaScript runtime that stores pitch continuously in hertz, encoding unquantized vocal glides, microtonal inflections, Bézier pitch contours, and media triggers as native temporal objects.

Cycla is a formal grammar and parser for nested, additive, and non-isochronous time structures. Compact .cyc expressions define rhythm trees and fractional phase shifts that compile into Tabota timelines.

Stanzuary extends the coordinate system from arranging musical events in time to arranging text specimens in three-dimensional space.

Xyh Tamura15

Practice architecture · 50 standalone browser works

The browser shelf, and the static-first rule 2009–2026

No build steps · zero external runtimes · inspectable source · archival permanence

Fifty projects across seventeen years run on a strict static-first architecture: vanilla HTML5, Web Audio, WebGL, CSS container queries, and local browser state. No build steps, no external frameworks, no bundled dependencies, and no server of my own anywhere in the stack; the pieces that read live data read public feeds directly from the browser. Every project opens as raw source and is engineered to outlive its hosting infrastructure.

This architecture articulates a conscious position: the practice is deliberately infrastructure-light, while the scientific phenomena it investigates (gravitational-wave interferometry, seismic arrays, multi-messenger coincidence) are infrastructure-maximal. Rather than simulating distributed network servers, the work runs classification and acoustic synthesis directly on the client machine—the browser as the primary instrument and analytical surface.
