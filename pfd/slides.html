Digital waveguide · physical modeling

The Unbounded Organ 2026

One waveguide specification evaluated across pipe lengths from 8.575 mm to 40,075 km · limits matrix and threshold readout

The Unbounded Organ is a digital waveguide organ synthesizer playable in the browser. It simulates an acoustic pipe across lengths ranging from 8.575 millimeters to the Earth's circumference (40,075 kilometers), generating audible pitches at small scales and slow infrasonic drifts across planetary distances.

An on-screen limits matrix reports physical acoustic cutoffs alongside digital sampling and memory constraints. The interface explicitly displays when a fundamental frequency exceeds physical or digital thresholds and reports which harmonic modes remain capable of radiating sound.

Optical media · microfacet transmission

Aeropane 2026

Frosted glass, gel, reeded glass, and grown ice modeled as a pane between the viewer and the image · offline progressive render

Aeropane is an image processing tool that renders images through simulated optical glass and rough surfaces (frosted glass, gel, reeded panels, and ice). The effect uses microfacet transmission physics to refract and blur a background picture using an editable height map, letting users import one image to serve as the refractive lens over another.

Scanning standards · atmospheric channel

W2XDX 2026

The receiving end of a television standard that never shipped, pulled in when the atmospheric duct opens

W2XDX is an interactive video synthesizer and television receiver simulation. It decodes broadcast video signals using alternate space-filling scan curves (standard raster lines, Hilbert curves, and random walks) transmitted through a simulated atmospheric channel, showing how broadcast noise and signal echoes manifest as distinct visual patterns on screen.

Detector aperture · particle game

Catchment 2026

Browser particle catcher, running also on a Raspberry Pi desk panel

Catchment is an arcade particle physics game playable in the browser and on a dedicated Raspberry Pi desktop panel. Players operate a detector aperture by toggling charge, parity, and spin settings to intercept incoming particles curved by a magnetic field.

Particles are caught only when all three quantum properties match the aperture's trigger state. The game illustrates how particle detector triggers filter physical events in real time: unselected particles pass through without leaving a record.

Live planetary data · browser instruments

Cytophones 2026

Seven single-file browser instruments · marine, seismic, and weather feeds read at run time

Cytophones is a collection of seven single-file browser instruments combining generative 2D graphics with real-time audio synthesis. Each instrument maps visual biological motion or live environmental telemetry directly into sound parameters.

Seven instruments connect to live planetary data feeds: Halocline drives ocean acoustics using marine buoy telemetry, Seismocyte synthesizes seismic textures from global USGS earthquake feeds, and Murmuration modulates generative soundscapes from live Manila weather data.

Three of the seven: a body detail, murmuration on live Manila weather, and cambium’s node-and-fiber graph.

Network poetics · DSP synthesis

a landline imagining a line probe during a storm 2026

ITU-T V.34 DSP handshake and line-probe degradation simulation

a landline imagining a line probe during a storm is an interactive sound piece and score simulating an ITU-T V.34 dial-up modem handshake across a degraded telephone line.

The audio synthesizer generates the authentic negotiation tones and line-probing signals of two modems measuring channel attenuation, phase jitter, and distortion in the presence of simulated electrical storm noise. The work treats the negotiation protocol as an acoustic instrument that establishes transmission parameters before data transfer begins.

Non-causal media processing

Hindcasts 2025–2026→

Hindcasts is a suite of offline audio and video tools that process a static file as a complete span, operating in both temporal directions before events arrive.

Remanence

An audio and video effect that simulates magnetic tape print-through, causing audio and image layers to bleed forward and backward across adjacent tape layers.

Metachamber

An offline reverb that detects upcoming silences between musical notes and shapes reverb decay tails to fit neatly into future pauses.

Pythia

A granular delay and sidechain processor that looks ahead in the audio timeline to trigger ducking and pitched grains prior to upcoming transients.

Prolepsis

A video feedback processor that computes motion trails across a video clip forwards, backwards, or symmetrically into future frames.

Sounder

A multiband dynamics processor that compiles a whole-file level histogram, allowing users to draw custom transfer curves without relying on envelope followers.

CyberScotoma

A video glitch effect that freezes selected screen regions and fills them with visual textures sampled from future frames in the video.

Hindcasts operates on entire audio and video files offline. Unlike real-time plugins that process sample-by-sample, these tools inspect the whole duration at once to anticipate future events, reverse decay tails, or apply non-causal statistical modulation.

Xyh Tamura8

Planetary acoustics · ray tracing delay

Gliese 2025–2026

Spherical acoustic ray tracing in graded-index atmospheres · Earth SOFAR channel and exoplanet presets

Gliese is an audio delay and reverb plugin for the browser based on spherical acoustic ray tracing. It simulates how sound waves bend and travel through layered planetary atmospheres and deep-ocean sound channels.

The tool includes presets for Earth's deep-ocean SOFAR channel and three exoplanetary atmospheres (Gliese 581g, Kepler 186f, HD 189733b). Acoustic reflection, refraction, and absorption profiles derived from atmospheric density and temperature gradients generate complex spatial delay patterns.

Equation-first synthesis

Anexacta 2026→

Five browser instruments on one mathematical-definition engine · Aliquoto, Cella, Moire, Physa, Spolium

Aliquoto

An additive synthesizer that builds overtone series from just-intonation intervals and mathematical ratios.

Cella

A physical modeling synthesizer generating sound from simulated two-dimensional wave propagation across cellular grids.

Moire

A phase-modulation synthesizer that converts spatial visual interference patterns into microtonal sideband audio.

Physa

A nonlinear synthesizer modeling an electrical memristor component with internal memory and state hysteresis.

Anexacta is a suite of five browser synthesizers driven by editable mathematical formulas rather than static presets. The instruments share a common evaluation engine, allowing harmonic spectra and physical models defined in one tool to be used across the others.

Formats and spatial notation

Tabota · Cycla 2025→

Open score format and notation language · continuous pitch representation · recursive metrical grammar

Tabota is an open plain-text score format and JavaScript playback engine. It stores pitch continuously in hertz rather than quantized MIDI semitones, natively preserving continuous vocal glides, microtonal inflections, and Bézier pitch curves.

Cycla is a rhythmic notation language and parser that compiles complex nested, additive, and polyrhythmic meter trees directly into Tabota timelines.

Stanzuary is an experimental 3D spatial editor for arranging literary and poetic texts along three spatial dimensions.
