Hydrangeas, malunggay, sunflowers, and photographed things lying in the grass in Forgetting are cutouts from Xyh Tamura's photographs for Foam Green City, licensed CC BY 4.0.

Modeled objects

The green Japanese public phone and wooden geta were modeled for this work with Codex. The tsinelas reuse Foam Green City's authored rubber slipper geometry. The footwear is rendered as cutouts for Forgetting. These authored objects are licensed CC BY 4.0.

In Hours, the banderitas reuse Foam Green City's authored bunting geometry. The parol stars, Japanese paper lanterns, and koinobori carp streamers were modeled for this work with Codex. These decorations are licensed CC BY 4.0.

Sound

The synth voices, chords, and tunings are written in Web Audio for these pieces. The tunings are equal temperament, just minor, septimal (7/6, 3/2, 7/4), and Whitewood35.

The opening page loops Xyh Tamura's theme, made with Kontakt factory library strings and two wind recordings, its ending crossfaded over its start.

Wind recordings from Winds of Nature, a royalty-free sound library by BOOM Library, cut into loops with the high frequencies lifted.
