Five Ways Through

It's time to be there now, it should be. I don't know if I'm who I'm supposed to be. I'm not sure what time it is. I just know I'll get there. I'll just know I'll get there and I'll have forgotten something.

Five short pieces among plants lit by a camera flash, played with a photographed hand. Each sentence opens one. They use sound, so headphones help. Works with a mouse or touch.
