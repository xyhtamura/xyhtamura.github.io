Xyh Tamura

Artist-researcher · singer-songwriter · creative technologist

Xyh Tamura is a Filipino/Ilonggo-Japanese composer, singer-songwriter, and artist-researcher based in Manila. The practice investigates voice, unquantized pitch notation, and physical acoustics across experimental pop, custom vocal instruments, browser synthesis engines, and musical theater. Rather than working inside supplied commercial formats, Xyh authors the apparatus: the acoustic instrument, notation language in hertz, recursive meter grammar, or digital waveguide model.

Desiderata, 2026.

Desiderata is an ambient vocal album built entirely from acoustic voice and breath recordings, layering time-stretched vocalizations, granulated textures, and microtonal vocoders against ocean and insect field audio. The companion xyhnthesizer is a Kontakt vocal organ sampled directly from the tracking sessions, mapping sustained phonemes and breath registers across keyboard zones.

The Unbounded Organ is a browser physical-modeling instrument that simulates acoustic organ pipe waveguides across lengths from 8.575 millimeters to 40,075 kilometers. By computing delay-line equations across extreme dimensions, the instrument demonstrates how a single acoustic model shifts from high musical pitches down to diffuse spatial echoes and planetary infrasonic frequencies.

A solo music practice spanning songwriting, vocal performance, synthesizer programming, algorithmic composition, and visual art.

Music, performance, and tools

Eosforos 2025–2026

Three-track release as xyhtochrome · Saronay, Kulintang, and Tambi by Tusa Montes · live audiovisual performance for Diana Aviado's Kaos Magick · “Theia” selected for The Wrong Eclipse

Eosforos is a three-track electronic music release and live audiovisual performance suite built with custom browser instruments. The music combines chaotic sound synthesis, microtonal vocal singing, and traditional Philippine percussion (Saronay, Kulintang, and Tambi recorded by Tusa Montes) to model the physical states of the early Hadean Earth. Live performances link audio playback directly to three custom web tools: the aperiodic oscillator Roil, the scale-free melody generator Antemelos, and the audio-reactive WebGL visualizer Hadean Flare.

Formats and spatial notation

Tabota · Cycla · Stanzuary 2025→

Open score format and notation language · recursive metrical grammar · spatial score and text editor

Tabota is a plain-text score format and JavaScript runtime that stores pitch continuously in hertz. It encodes unquantized vocal glides, microtonal inflections, Bézier pitch contours, spatial trajectories, and media triggers as temporal objects.

Cycla is a formal grammar and parser for nested, additive, and non-isochronous time structures. Compact .cyc expressions define rhythm trees and fractional phase shifts that compile into Tabota timelines.

Stanzuary is a 3D WebGL score and text editor that arranges musical events, phonemes, and text fragments across a navigable three-dimensional coordinate space.

Browser instruments · physical simulation

Cytophones 2026

Family of seven browser instruments in which 2D physics simulations drive Web Audio synthesis

A suite of seven browser instruments where 2D physics simulations control real-time Web Audio sound synthesis. Players manipulate fluid boundaries, cellular membranes, flocking particle swarms, and stomatal pores on canvas, with collisions and structural deformations modulating resonators, delay lines, and waveguide models.

Philippine timbres and environmental data feeds inform the synthesis architecture: ocean swell models, tidal ranges, and seismic telemetry perturb the physical membranes, turning continuous simulation into immediate, gestural acoustic performance.

Song and presentation

Night Bus · intermedia musicopoetics 2025

Song within Of Another Shore · presented at Saliksik-Musika II, University of the Philippines, June 2025

Night Bus is an electroacoustic song and performance paper from the intermedia suite Of Another Shore. The musical work combines Tagalog vocal gestures, microtonal synthesizer ostinatos, and granular transit engine noise to trace nocturnal commuter routes across Metro Manila. The accompanying research paper presented at Saliksik-Musika II analyzes how historical Tagalog rowing songs (soliranin) and street vendor chants (indolanin) persist as rhythmic structures in contemporary public transit.

Film and live score

Manifest 2024–2025

Short film and videopoem by Bea Mariano and Xyh Tamura · live score by Xyh Tamura · presented at Search Mindscape Foundation and WHYNoT

Manifest is a 12-minute short film, videopoem, and live performance score created with filmmaker Bea Mariano. The film montages deep-sea footage of the USS Samuel B. Roberts shipwreck, Metro Manila high-rise construction, microbial growth, and historical military reels. The live performance score pairs analog synthesizers, voice, and hydrophone recordings, generating asynchronous drones and rhythmic pulses that respond to screen cuts.

Screened to an audience, and at WHYNoT with the printed programme.

Musical theater

The Magic Staff 1999

Meralco Theatre · conceived and written at age nine · librettist, songwriter, and lead actor · with Fides Cuyugan-Asensio, Bernardo Bernardo, Monique Wilson, and other Philippine theater artists

Equation-first synthesis

Anexacta 2026→

Anexacta is a collection of browser instruments that turn mathematical and physical models into playable sound synthesizers.

Aliquoto

A browser synthesizer that generates harmonic and inharmonic timbres by distributing additive partials across just-intonation intervals and geometric series.

Cella

A 2D wave reverberator that simulates wave propagation across coupled cellular grid meshes to produce spatial acoustic decay.

Moire

An optical sound generator that converts visual interference fringes and overlapping spatial frequencies into microtonal FM sidebands.

Physa

A nonlinear synthesizer that models memristive state transitions to inject analog hysteresis, resistance memory, and harmonic distortion into the signal path.
